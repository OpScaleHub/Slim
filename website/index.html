Engineered for Focus

Every interaction designed to be swift, beautiful, and completely custom.

The Wave Scroll Gesture

No more searching for app icons. Swipe along either edge of your screen. The alphabetical index bends organically under your thumb, launching any application instantly with subtle, mechanical haptic vibrations.

100% Privacy, No Telemetry

Slim does not collect app usage, crash reports, or search inputs. Everything runs offline — the only network call it can ever make is the optional, opt-in weather fetch from Open-Meteo, and only if you enable it.

Dynamic Color Theming

Slim supports Android's Material You theme engine. The launcher colors, text indicators, and folder icons adapt automatically to match your wallpaper palette.

Inline Notification Previews

See the latest message notifications right below your favorite app shortcuts — know what's waiting before you even open the app or the notification tray.

Smart Floating Search

Swipe up and start typing. Results are ranked by relevance — exact starts first, then word matches, then anything containing your query. Recently searched apps appear as quick-launch chips, no favorites required.

Make It Yours

Toggle the clock, date, and weather independently. Pick 12 or 24-hour time, °C or °F, offline or real weather. Settings live inside the "Slim" app entry itself — no buttons cluttering your home screen.
